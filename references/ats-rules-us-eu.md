# ATS Rules by Market

## US Resume

- no photo
- no age, birth date, gender, or marital status
- no full address required
- 1 to 2 pages for most roles
- lead with impact, scale, and keywords
- use a clean, parser-friendly layout

## UK CV

- keep personal details minimal
- include location and work authorization only if useful
- include a professional summary
- keep bullets evidence-based
- adapt language to local hiring norms

## EU CV

- keep personal details minimal unless locally expected
- include languages when relevant
- mention work authorization or relocation only if useful
- keep formatting simple for ATS parsing

## Universal ATS Rules

- mirror the target job language when truthful
- do not keyword stuff
- prefer standard section names
- use concrete verbs and metrics
- keep dates consistent
- avoid tables, text boxes, and decorative layouts in ATS versions
