# Market Positioning Templates

## US

- title should be concise and keyword-rich
- summary should show impact and scope
- bullets should lead with outcomes
- use plain, parser-friendly formatting

## UK

- keep the role title aligned with the target market
- use a clear profile summary
- mention languages and work authorization when relevant
- keep the CV clean and compact

## EU

- use local role naming where appropriate
- include languages when useful
- mention relocation or remote preference if relevant
- keep the document easy to parse

## LinkedIn

- headline should combine role + key expertise + value
- about section should summarize positioning and proof
- experience bullets should mirror resume claims

## Recruiter Message

- state target role
- mention one or two strongest signals
- show fit in one sentence
- keep it short and specific
