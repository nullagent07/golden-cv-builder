# Intake Questionnaire

Use only the questions needed for the current task.

1. What is the target market: US, UK, EU, global, or a specific country?
2. What is the target level: junior, mid, senior, lead, or staff?
3. What is the goal: positioning, resume rewrite, CV adaptation, LinkedIn, or job application?
4. Do you want a conservative positioning or the strongest defensible positioning?
5. Do you have a resume, job description, stack list, or all three?
